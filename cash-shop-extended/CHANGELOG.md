# Changelog

## 3.1.0
- The Cash Shop uses all its tabs: New (auras, footprints and effects), Popular (costumes), Limited Sale (monthly and special boxes), Permanent Equipment, Scrolls, Consumables and Other. The items and prices are unchanged. Every app version this mod supports (1.6.0 and later) shows each tab's own items, so the one-tab workaround is no longer needed.

## 3.0.0
- New tab: 102 more items, mostly footprint and aura effects (Purple Star, Nyar, Divine Light, Ghost, Twinkle, Blossom, ...) at 50, plus a few iRO extras such as the Nebula Orb and the Glacier follower at 500.
- Removed: Neuralizer, AllMighty_Up, Glacier Whip, two Mad Bunny enchants and the Fixed Casting reduction.
- Prices: Enriched Etherdeocon 3 → 1, Blacksmith Blessing 5 → 1, Enriched Elunium Box 5 → 1, HD Carnium Box (10) 5 → 1, HD Oridecon Box (50) 3 → 5, Shadow AllMighty Earring and Pendant 300 → 100.
- Needs app 1.6.0 or later.
